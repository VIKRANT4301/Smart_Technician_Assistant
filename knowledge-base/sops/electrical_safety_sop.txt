=== ELECTRICAL CONTROL CABINET SOP: MODEL SOP-ELEC-04 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Operating Voltage: 220V - 440V AC
- Enclosure Rating: NEMA 4X
- Main Breaker Limit: 100A

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – ELECTRICAL FAULTS:
Symptom: Cabinet breaker trips, machine fails to start, loose contact indicator glows.
Possible Causes:
1. Loose cable connection: Loose contact near terminal blocks causes circuit breakdown or high resistance.
2. Capacitor dielectric degradation: Swelling or failure of start/run capacitor.
3. Contactor coil failure: Damage to magnetic starter.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Lockout/Tagout the electrical control cabinet feeding the machine.
2. Verify the circuit is completely dead using a calibrated non-contact voltage tester.
3. Locate loose cables near terminal block 4, re-insert them, and tighten the clamp screws.
4. Discharge the start/run capacitors using an insulated resistor tool.
5. Disconnect the capacitor leads and test capacitance with a multimeter.
6. Replace capacitor if the reading is ±10% outside the nominal rating.

SAFETY MANDATES:
- Wear Class E insulated safety gloves (rated up to 1000V) and safety glasses.
- Remove all metal jewelry, rings, and watches before inspection.
- Do not perform live-voltage tests alone.
