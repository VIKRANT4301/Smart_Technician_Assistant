=== CENTRIFUGAL PUMP REPAIR GUIDE: MODEL CP-100 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Operating Temperature: 32°F to 150°F
- Normal Operating Pressure: 45 PSI - 80 PSI
- Pump Type: Centrifugal Pump Unit
- Gasket Type: Nitrile O-Ring

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – PUMP LEAKAGE:
Symptom: Fluid dripping near pump base plate, casing pressure drops.
Possible Causes:
1. Worn Mechanical Seal / Gland Packing: Friction and high operating hours degrade packing rings or ceramic faces of the seal.
2. Damaged O-Rings or Casing Gaskets: Chemical incompatibility or thermal cycles cause elastomeric seals to split.
3. Shaft Misalignment: Creates eccentric rotation which destroys seals.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Isolate and De-pressurize: Shut down power using LOTO. Close inlet and outlet valves. Open bleed valve to drain remaining pressure.
2. Safety Measures: If pumping chemicals or hot fluid, wear chemical-resistant gloves, apron, and full face shield.
3. Disassemble Casing: Undo casing bolts in a cross-pattern. Remove front cover to inspect gaskets.
4. Replace Gland Packing or Seals: Remove old packing rings, clean packing bore, install new pre-cut packing rings, offsetting joints by 90 degrees.
5. Realign and Reassemble: Inspect alignment with a dial indicator. Reassemble casing with a new gasket.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:
- Lubricate pump bearings every 3 months.
- Check alignment using dial indicator monthly.
