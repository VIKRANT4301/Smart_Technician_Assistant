=== LAPTOP REPAIR GUIDE: MODEL LT-PRO X15 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Fan Speed Range: 2000 RPM - 5500 RPM
- Heat Pipe Count: 3 copper tubes
- Battery Voltage Rating: 11.4V DC
- Manufacturer: ASUS

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – THERMAL & FAN FAULTS:
Symptom: Cooling fan makes grinding noises, drops RPM under load, CPU temperature thermal throttles.
Possible Causes:
1. Dust Clogged Heat Pipe Fins: Accumulation blockages at the heatsink exit ports.
2. Dried Thermal Compound: Hardened CPU/GPU thermal grease.
3. Fan Bearing Wear: Lost lubrication in the fan spindle sleeve.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Safe Shutdown: Shut down the laptop, disconnect the charger, unscrew the bottom chassis cover, and immediately disconnect the battery cable.
2. Heatsink Module Disassembly: Remove the heatsink mounting screws in reverse numeric order (4-3-2-1). Lift the heatsink from CPU and GPU.
3. Thermal Compound Refresh: Clean the chip dies and heatsink plate using isopropyl alcohol and lint-free wipes. Apply a thin layer of thermal compound (non-conductive) evenly.
4. Fan Replacements: Unscrew the dual fan modules, unplug their ribbon connectors from the motherboard, and install new replacement fans.
5. Reassembly and Test: Reattach the heatsink, tighten screws in order (1-2-3-4), reconnect the battery cable, close the cover, and run a thermal diagnostic stress test.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:
- Clean fan dust using compressed air quarterly.
- Avoid blockages by keeping laptop on solid tables during high CPU workloads.
