=== HVAC COMPRESSOR REPAIR GUIDE: MODEL AC-X300 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Gasket Type: Neoprene Seal
- Pressure Relief Setpoint: 450 PSI
- Torque Specifications: Casing bolts 25 ft-lbs
- Manufacturer: Samsung

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – REPAIR DIAGNOSTICS:
Symptom: High head pressure, rattling sound from compressor, poor cooling output.
Possible Causes:
1. Damaged Compressor Mounting: Loose mounting bolts or worn vibration isolators.
2. Contaminated Refrigerant: Moisture or air in the system piping.
3. Internal Scroll Damage: Mechanical wear inside the hermetic scroll compressor.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Discharge and Recovery: Recover all R-410A refrigerant from the system using an EPA-certified recovery unit. Do not vent to atmosphere.
2. Casing Bolt Torqueing: Inspect compressor casing mounting bolts. Tighten to specification (25 ft-lbs) using a calibrated torque wrench.
3. Filter Drier Replacement: Cut out the old liquid line filter drier and braze a new one in place while purging with dry nitrogen.
4. Vacuum Evacuation: Pull a vacuum on the system to at least 500 microns. Hold for 15 minutes to verify zero moisture or leaks.
5. Refrigerant Recharging: Charge system with R-410A by weight according to nameplate specifications using a digital scale.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:
- Inspect mounting rubber pads annually.
- Check compressor amp draw during high heat loads.
