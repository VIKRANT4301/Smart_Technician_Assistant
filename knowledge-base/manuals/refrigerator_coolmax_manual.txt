=== REFRIGERATOR SERVICE MANUAL: MODEL COOLMAX-R10 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Compartment Temperature: 34°F to 38°F
- Freezer Temperature: -2°F to 2°F
- Compressor Type: Variable Speed Inverter
- Refrigerant Type: R-600a
- Manufacturer: Whirlpool

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – INADEQUATE COOLING:
Symptom: Refrigerator compartment temperature exceeds 45°F, compressor runs constantly or clicks.
Possible Causes:
1. Dirty Condenser Coils: Dust accumulation on under-unit coils prevents proper heat rejection, resulting in compressor overload.
2. Faulty Defrost Thermostat: Frost builds up on evaporator coils, blocking cold airflow.
3. Damaged Door Gasket: Worn or torn magnetic seal allows warm ambient air to leak inside.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Safe Shutdown: Unplug the refrigerator power cord from the wall outlet before starting service.
2. Coil Cleaning: Use a condenser coil brush and vacuum cleaner to remove dust and pet hair from under-unit coils.
3. Defrost System Check: Use a multimeter to test continuity of the defrost heater and defrost thermostat. Replace faulty parts.
4. Gasket Inspection: Check the magnetic door gasket for tears or air leaks. Clean the gasket with warm water or replace if damaged.
5. Air Vent Clearing: Ensure internal air vents between freezer and refrigerator sections are not blocked by food packages.

SAFETY MANDATES:
- Disconnect power supply before opening panel covers or touching electrical connections.
- Wear cut-resistant gloves when handling metal panel edges and coil fins.
