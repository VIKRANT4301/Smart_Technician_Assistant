=== HVAC COMPRESSOR MANUAL: MODEL AC-X200 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Operating Temperature: 45°F to 125°F
- Normal Current Draw: 12A - 15A
- Compressor Type: Hermetic Scroll
- Coolant: R-410A
- Manufacturer: Samsung

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE - COMPRESSOR OVERHEATING:
Symptom: Compressor surface temperature exceeds 180°F, high thermal overload switch trips.
Possible Causes:
1. Blocked Condenser Coils: Dirt, leaves, or debris blocking ventilation causes excessive heat accumulation, resulting in motor overheating.
2. Low Refrigerant Charge: System running low on R-410A leads to insufficient cooling of the compressor motor itself.
3. Faulty Run Capacitor: A weak capacitor causes high winding currents and elevated motor temperature.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Safe Shutdown: Turn off power at the main circuit breaker before starting any inspection.
2. Coil Cleaning: Spray commercial coil cleaner on condenser coils, let it sit for 5 minutes, and rinse with low-pressure water. Clear any debris within a 2-foot radius.
3. Electrical Check: Inspect run capacitor for swelling or leakage. Test capacitance with a multimeter. Replace if capacitance is ±10% outside rating.
4. Refrigerant Check: Hook up pressure gauges to check suction and discharge pressures. Subcooling should be around 10-12°F.
5. Ventilation Inspection: Clean air vents and inspect the condenser cooling fan. Ensure the fan motor is spinning freely.

SAFETY MANDATES:
- Wear safety goggles and heavy-duty work gloves when handling refrigerant or pressurized lines.
- Use Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) protocols to ensure electrical circuits cannot be accidentally energized.
