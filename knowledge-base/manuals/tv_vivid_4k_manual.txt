=== SMART TV SERVICE MANUAL: MODEL VIVID-4K ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Panel Size: 55" LED Backlight UHD
- Power Supply Rating: 100V - 240V AC
- Audio Output: 20W Dolby Atmos
- Backlight Type: Direct LED
- Manufacturer: LG

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – NO IMAGE / BLACK SCREEN:
Symptom: Power LED indicator flashes, sound works, but screen displays no image (black screen).
Possible Causes:
1. Failed LED Backlight Strips: Burned out LED segments inside the panel disable the backlight.
2. Blown T-CON Board Fuse: Short circuit on the timing controller board shuts down panel power.
3. Loose LVDS/Ribbon Cables: Unsecured connections between the mainboard and the display panel.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Safe Shutdown: Unplug the TV power cord from the electrical socket and wait 5 minutes to allow capacitors to discharge.
2. Flashlight Test: Shine a flashlight close to the screen while playing video. If faint images are visible, the LED backlight circuit is faulty.
3. Backlight Strips Replacement: Disassemble the screen panel carefully, test individual LED strips with a backlight tester, and replace damaged strips.
4. Timing Controller (T-CON) Check: Measure input voltage on the T-CON board fuse. If the fuse is blown, replace the T-CON board.
5. Ribbon Cable Re-seating: Disconnect, clean with contact cleaner, and firmly re-seat all internal LVDS ribbon cables.

SAFETY MANDATES:
- High voltage warning: Never touch the power supply board components when the TV is plugged in.
- Wear anti-static ESD wrist straps when handling sensitive logic boards.
