=== GRINDING MACHINE MANUAL: MODEL GM-PRO300 ===
SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:
- Operating Temperature: 50°F to 110°F
- Spindle Thread: 5/8"-11 UNC
- Wheel Diameter: 7 inches
- Rated Power: 15A / 120V
- No-Load Speed: 6000 RPM

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – WHEEL VIBRATION & OVERHEATING:
Symptom: High vibration during grinding operations, or surface motor casing overheating.
Possible Causes:
1. Cracked or Damaged Wheel: Structural cracks in the abrasive wheel create massive unbalance.
2. Loose Mounting Flange: Spindle nut not torqued down, causing wheel slippage and vibration.
3. Worn Motor Brushes: Worn-out carbon brushes cause electrical arcing and motor casing overheating.

RESOLUTION STEPS:
1. Safe Isolation: Power off the grinding machine. Disconnect from mains outlet and isolate the plug inside a lockbox following LOTO.
2. Guard check: Inspect safety guard position. Ensure guard is secured and covers at least 180 degrees of the wheel.
3. Flange check: Check spindle nut and flange torque. Tighten spindle nut using the dual-pin spanner wrench.
4. Wheel Replacement: Replace grinding wheel if hairline cracks or chipping is visible. Do NOT exceed max speed rated for the wheel.
5. Brush Replacement: Unscrew carbon brush caps, slide out old carbon brushes, insert new brushes, and secure caps.
6. Spindle Inspection: Check spindle shaft using a dial indicator. Runout should be less than 0.05 mm.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:
- Check safety guard alignment before every shift.
- Blow out motor casing with dry compressed air weekly to remove metal dust.
- Inspect power cord for cuts or exposed wires.
