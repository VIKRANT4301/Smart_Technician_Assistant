=== LAPTOP SERVICE MANUAL: MODEL LT-PRO X15 ===

SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS:

* Display Size: 15.6" Full HD IPS
* Processor: Intel Core i7 13th Gen
* RAM: 16GB DDR5
* Storage: 512GB NVMe SSD
* Battery Capacity: 68Wh Lithium-Ion
* Operating Temperature: 50°F to 95°F
* Cooling System: Dual-Fan Heat Pipe
* Power Adapter: 90W USB-C Fast Charging

TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE – LAPTOP OVERHEATING:

Symptom:
Laptop chassis becomes excessively hot, cooling fans run continuously at high speed, and the system may throttle performance or shut down unexpectedly.

Possible Causes:

1. Blocked Air Vents:
   Dust accumulation or obstruction around intake/exhaust vents restricts airflow, causing internal heat buildup.

2. Thermal Paste Degradation:
   Dried or poorly applied CPU/GPU thermal paste reduces heat transfer efficiency between processor and heatsink.

3. High Background Resource Usage:
   Multiple heavy applications or malware processes can drive CPU/GPU usage to 100%, increasing operating temperatures.

4. Faulty Cooling Fan:
   Damaged or slow-spinning fan prevents proper heat dissipation from internal components.

5. Incorrect Power Settings:
   High-performance mode may force the processor to operate continuously at maximum clock speeds.

RESOLUTION STEPS:

1. Safe Shutdown:
   Power off the laptop completely and disconnect the charger before performing maintenance.

2. Vent Cleaning:
   Use compressed air to clean dust from intake and exhaust vents. Maintain at least 6 inches of clearance around airflow openings.

3. Fan Inspection:
   Check whether cooling fans spin freely and produce normal airflow. Replace fans showing abnormal noise or inconsistent speed.

4. Thermal Paste Replacement:
   Remove heatsink assembly, clean old thermal compound using isopropyl alcohol, and apply a fresh thin layer of thermal paste before reinstallation.

5. Software Diagnostics:
   Open Task Manager and inspect CPU/GPU utilization. Disable unnecessary startup applications and scan the system for malware.

6. Power Configuration:
   Switch system power mode from “High Performance” to “Balanced” to reduce excessive thermal output.

7. Temperature Verification:
   Use hardware monitoring software to confirm CPU temperatures remain below 185°F (85°C) during normal operation.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:

* Clean ventilation openings every 3 months.
* Avoid using the laptop on soft surfaces such as beds or cushions.
* Keep BIOS and drivers updated regularly.
* Store and operate the laptop in dry, dust-free environments.
