Samsung HVAC Compressor Model: AC-X900-SUPER
This is the official troubleshooting guide for the Samsung AC-X900-SUPER HVAC unit.
Troubleshooting Guidelines
If the compressor runs hot, check the condenser coils and run capacitor.
Resolution Steps
1. Shut down power at the main circuit breaker labeling AC-MAIN-900.
2. Clean the condenser fan blades using soft brush.
